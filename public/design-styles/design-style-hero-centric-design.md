# Hero-Centric Design

## Best for
General users who need a strong first impression.

## Core idea
Make the top of the page do most of the communication work.

## Above-the-fold layout
- Large visual hero area
- Clear headline and short subheading
- Primary call-to-action
- Supporting image, illustration, or product mockup

## Visual hierarchy notes
- The hero should explain the page in seconds.
- Keep the first screen focused.
- Use imagery to support the message, not replace it.

## Works well for
Campaign pages, product launches, portfolios, events, and promotional websites.
