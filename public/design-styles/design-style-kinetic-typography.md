# Kinetic Typography

## Best for
Young or creative audiences who respond to motion, rhythm, and expressive type.

## Core idea
Let typography move, shift, scale, or reveal itself to create energy and guide attention.

## Above-the-fold layout
- Animated or highly expressive headline
- Short supporting message
- Motion-led transition or reveal
- Simple call-to-action

## Visual hierarchy notes
- Motion should emphasize the message, not hide it.
- Keep animation timing short and readable.
- Use movement to direct the eye through the page.

## Works well for
Event pages, music, fashion, campaign sites, creative portfolios, and experimental brand pages.
