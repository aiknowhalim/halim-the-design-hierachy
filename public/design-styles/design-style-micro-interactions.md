# Micro-interactions

## Best for
Beginner and creative audiences who need feedback, encouragement, and clear interface responses.

## Core idea
Use small animations and responses to confirm actions, reveal state, and make the interface feel alive.

## Above-the-fold layout
- Clear headline
- Interactive button or card
- Tiny hover, click, or progress feedback
- Supporting labels that explain the interaction

## Visual hierarchy notes
- Motion should guide attention, not compete with content.
- Use interactions to confirm meaning.
- Keep animations short and purposeful.

## Works well for
Apps, onboarding flows, product demos, learning tools, and creative portfolios.
