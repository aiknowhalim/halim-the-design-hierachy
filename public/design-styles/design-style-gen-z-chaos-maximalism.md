# Gen Z Chaos / Maximalism

## Best for
Young or creative audiences who respond to expressive, internet-native energy.

## Core idea
Use layered type, stickers, bold colors, mixed media, and controlled visual overload to create attitude.

## Above-the-fold layout
- Huge headline with attitude
- Stickers, badges, or layered shapes
- Multiple small visual cues
- Clear main action so the chaos still has direction

## Visual hierarchy notes
- Make one element clearly dominant.
- Use visual overload as personality, not confusion.
- Keep navigation and calls-to-action easy to find.

## Works well for
Creator brands, music, fashion, youth campaigns, culture sites, and experimental launches.
