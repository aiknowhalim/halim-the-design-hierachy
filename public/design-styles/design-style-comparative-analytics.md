# Comparative Analytics

## Best for
Expert audiences who need to compare options, patterns, or outcomes.

## Core idea
Make differences visible through side-by-side structure, benchmarks, tables, scorecards, and clear labels.

## Above-the-fold layout
- Comparison-led headline
- Two or more option panels
- Key metric or benchmark row
- Clear takeaway statement

## Visual hierarchy notes
- Use consistent categories across comparisons.
- Highlight the most important difference.
- Make the conclusion visible, not hidden in the data.

## Works well for
Reports, product comparisons, research summaries, decision tools, dashboards, and strategy pages.
