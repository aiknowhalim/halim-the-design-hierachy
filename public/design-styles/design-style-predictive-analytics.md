# Predictive Analytics

## Best for
Expert audiences who need to understand trends, forecasts, risk, or likely outcomes.

## Core idea
Use forward-looking visuals such as projections, confidence ranges, trend lines, and scenario cards.

## Above-the-fold layout
- Forecast-focused headline
- Main trend or projection graphic
- Scenario cards
- Key assumption or confidence note

## Visual hierarchy notes
- Separate facts from predictions.
- Label uncertainty clearly.
- Make the most likely outcome easy to identify.
- Avoid visual complexity that hides the takeaway.

## Works well for
Analytics tools, finance, operations, research, planning dashboards, and expert reports.
