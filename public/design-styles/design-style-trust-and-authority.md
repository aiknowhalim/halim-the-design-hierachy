# Trust & Authority

## Best for
Professional or business audiences who need credibility and proof.

## Core idea
Build confidence through structured information, proof points, testimonials, credentials, and calm visual polish.

## Above-the-fold layout
- Confident headline
- Outcome-focused subheading
- Proof strip with logos, metrics, or testimonials
- Professional call-to-action

## Visual hierarchy notes
- Put evidence close to claims.
- Use calm colors, consistent spacing, and clean typography.
- Avoid gimmicks that reduce credibility.

## Works well for
Consulting, SaaS, finance, education, healthcare, enterprise services, and B2B websites.
