# Interactive Cursor Design

## Best for
Creative audiences who enjoy playful, exploratory interfaces.

## Core idea
Use the cursor as part of the experience through hover effects, reveal states, magnetic buttons, or custom movement.

## Above-the-fold layout
- Clear headline
- Interactive hero area
- Cursor-reactive visual element
- Obvious instruction or cue

## Visual hierarchy notes
- Do not hide core content behind cursor effects.
- Keep interactions optional, not required.
- Make sure the design still works on touch screens.

## Works well for
Creative portfolios, agency sites, interactive art pages, event sites, and experimental product launches.
