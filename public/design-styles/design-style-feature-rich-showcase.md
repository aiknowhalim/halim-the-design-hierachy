# Feature-Rich Showcase

## Best for
Professional or business audiences comparing capabilities.

## Core idea
Present many features without making the page feel cluttered by grouping related information into clear sections.

## Above-the-fold layout
- Product promise headline
- Feature preview cards
- Short benefit statements
- Visual product screenshot or diagram

## Visual hierarchy notes
- Group features by user goal.
- Use icons and labels to improve scanning.
- Highlight the most valuable feature first.

## Works well for
SaaS, tools, platforms, productivity products, and enterprise websites.
