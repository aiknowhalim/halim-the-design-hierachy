# Bento Grids

## Best for
Business and expert audiences who need modular, scannable information.

## Core idea
Use a card-based grid with different card sizes to show priority and organize related ideas.

## Above-the-fold layout
- Large feature card for the main message
- Smaller proof or detail cards
- Consistent spacing
- Clear labels and visual anchors

## Visual hierarchy notes
- Use card size to communicate importance.
- Keep each card focused on one point.
- Avoid making every card equally loud.

## Works well for
SaaS websites, portfolios, product explainers, dashboards, and design guides.
