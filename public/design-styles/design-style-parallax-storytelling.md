# Parallax Storytelling

## Best for
Beginner and creative audiences who benefit from guided, memorable movement.

## Core idea
Use layered scrolling effects to reveal information gradually and create a sense of progression.

## Above-the-fold layout
- Immersive opening scene
- Short narrative headline
- Layered background or illustration
- Scroll cue
- First chapter or starting action

## Visual hierarchy notes
- Motion should clarify sequence, not distract.
- Keep text readable over moving layers.
- Use parallax sparingly on important transitions.

## Works well for
Campaigns, education, brand stories, museum projects, travel pages, and creative portfolios.
