# Minimal & Direct

## Best for
General users who need to understand the page quickly.

## Core idea
Use one clear message, one obvious action, and very few distractions.

## Above-the-fold layout
- Short navigation
- Large direct headline
- One sentence of support copy
- One main call-to-action button
- Simple visual or product preview

## Visual hierarchy notes
- Make the headline the strongest element.
- Use generous spacing.
- Keep color limited to the action or key highlight.
- Avoid decorative elements that do not support the message.

## Works well for
Landing pages, service pages, personal websites, simple product explainers, and public information pages.
