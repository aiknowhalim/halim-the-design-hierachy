# Social Proof-Focused

## Best for
General and business audiences who need reassurance before taking action.

## Core idea
Use testimonials, logos, ratings, case studies, and outcome numbers to make trust visible.

## Above-the-fold layout
- Benefit-led headline
- Testimonial or rating near the hero
- Client logos or proof strip
- Clear call-to-action

## Visual hierarchy notes
- Put proof close to the decision point.
- Use real numbers when possible.
- Make testimonials scannable with names, roles, and outcomes.

## Works well for
Services, SaaS, consulting, education, ecommerce, and portfolio websites.
