# Inclusive Design

## Best for
Beginner, older, and accessibility-focused audiences.

## Core idea
Design for a wide range of people, devices, abilities, reading levels, and contexts from the start.

## Above-the-fold layout
- Clear headline
- Plain-language explanation
- Large readable text
- Obvious controls
- Multiple cues beyond color

## Visual hierarchy notes
- Support keyboard, touch, and screen-reader use.
- Use strong structure and clear headings.
- Keep layouts predictable and forgiving.

## Works well for
Public services, education, healthcare, community projects, nonprofit sites, and broad-audience products.
