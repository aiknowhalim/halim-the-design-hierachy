# Storytelling-Driven

## Best for
Beginner audiences who need context and a guided learning path.

## Core idea
Turn the page into a journey. Introduce the problem, build understanding, and reveal details in a clear sequence.

## Above-the-fold layout
- Chapter-style label
- Warm headline
- Short setup paragraph
- Visual cue showing progress or journey
- Button that invites people to begin

## Visual hierarchy notes
- Use section numbers or chapters.
- Add examples before technical detail.
- Use pacing, spacing, and narrative transitions.

## Works well for
Education pages, nonprofits, portfolios, campaigns, documentaries, and concept explanations.
