# Conversion-Optimized

## Best for
General users who need a clear next step.

## Core idea
Guide attention toward a decision, signup, purchase, booking, or inquiry.

## Above-the-fold layout
- Benefit-led headline
- Short supporting proof
- Primary call-to-action
- Secondary reassurance, such as reviews or guarantees

## Visual hierarchy notes
- Make the main action visually dominant.
- Remove competing links near the decision point.
- Use proof near the call-to-action.
- Repeat the action after important sections.

## Works well for
Landing pages, sign-up pages, campaign pages, services, and lead-generation sites.
