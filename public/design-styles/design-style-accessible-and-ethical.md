# Accessible & Ethical

## Best for
Accessibility-focused audiences and broad public audiences.

## Core idea
Make the interface understandable, usable, and respectful for people with different abilities, devices, and contexts.

## Above-the-fold layout
- Clear heading structure
- High contrast text
- Plain-language explanation
- Obvious button and link states
- No hidden or manipulative patterns

## Visual hierarchy notes
- Do not rely on color alone.
- Make focus states visible.
- Use readable type sizes and spacing.
- Keep navigation predictable.

## Works well for
Public services, education, healthcare, civic projects, nonprofit websites, and inclusive product experiences.
