# Chromatic Aberration / RGB Split

## Best for
Creative audiences that enjoy digital, edgy, or experimental visuals.

## Core idea
Use subtle red, green, and blue offset effects to create a glitchy, high-energy digital feeling.

## Above-the-fold layout
- Bold headline with RGB split treatment
- Dark or saturated background
- Glitch-inspired visual accent
- Clear readable supporting text

## Visual hierarchy notes
- Keep the effect subtle enough to preserve readability.
- Apply it to hero graphics or display text, not long paragraphs.
- Pair experimental visuals with simple layout structure.

## Works well for
Music, gaming, digital art, tech culture, festivals, and experimental portfolios.
