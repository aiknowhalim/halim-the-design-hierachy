# Memphis Design

## Best for
Young or creative audiences who enjoy expressive, high-energy visuals.

## Core idea
Use bold geometry, playful shapes, clashing colors, and unexpected composition to create a memorable personality.

## Above-the-fold layout
- Oversized expressive headline
- Bright background or color blocks
- Decorative geometric shapes
- Short message with a clear action

## Visual hierarchy notes
- Keep the main message readable despite the visual energy.
- Use shape and color to frame the headline.
- Balance chaos with clear navigation and section order.

## Works well for
Creative portfolios, event pages, youth campaigns, music, fashion, culture, and experimental brand sites.
