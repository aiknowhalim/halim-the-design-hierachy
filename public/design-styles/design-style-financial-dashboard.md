# Financial Dashboard

## Best for
Business and expert audiences who need dense information organized clearly.

## Core idea
Use charts, metric cards, tables, and status indicators to make complex performance data scannable.

## Above-the-fold layout
- KPI row or metric summary
- Main chart or comparison panel
- Secondary data cards
- Clear filters or time ranges

## Visual hierarchy notes
- Put the most important number first.
- Use consistent labels and units.
- Separate critical signals from supporting data.

## Works well for
Finance, analytics, operations, reporting tools, admin panels, and expert-facing dashboards.
