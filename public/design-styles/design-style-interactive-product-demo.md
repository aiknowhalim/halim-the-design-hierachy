# Interactive Product Demo

## Best for
Beginner and general users who understand better by trying or seeing.

## Core idea
Show the product or concept in action instead of only describing it.

## Above-the-fold layout
- Clear product promise
- Interactive preview or demo panel
- Short explanation of what to try
- Call-to-action after the demo

## Visual hierarchy notes
- Make the demo area visually dominant.
- Label interactive parts clearly.
- Give immediate feedback when users interact.

## Works well for
Software products, learning tools, calculators, onboarding pages, and feature explainers.
