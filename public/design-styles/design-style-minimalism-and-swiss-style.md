# Minimalism & Swiss Style

## Best for
Expert audiences who value precision, structure, and fast scanning.

## Core idea
Use disciplined grids, strong alignment, functional typography, and generous space to make complex information feel clear.

## Above-the-fold layout
- Grid-aligned headline
- Clear supporting statement
- Structured data or proof block
- Minimal navigation
- One precise call-to-action

## Visual hierarchy notes
- Let layout structure carry the design.
- Use type scale and spacing instead of decoration.
- Keep color restrained and purposeful.
- Make complex content easy to compare.

## Works well for
Research pages, expert guides, analytical products, editorial design, portfolios, and professional websites.
