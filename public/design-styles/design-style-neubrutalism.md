# Neubrutalism

## Best for
Young, creative, and bold brands that want a raw, confident, high-contrast look.

## Core idea
Use thick borders, flat colors, hard shadows, strong type, and intentionally rough composition.

## Above-the-fold layout
- Large bold headline
- Chunky buttons
- High-contrast color blocks
- Card elements with thick outlines

## Visual hierarchy notes
- Use scale and contrast aggressively.
- Keep the layout simple so the style does not become messy.
- Let buttons and labels feel obvious and tactile.

## Works well for
Creative portfolios, indie products, culture sites, startup launches, and experimental landing pages.
