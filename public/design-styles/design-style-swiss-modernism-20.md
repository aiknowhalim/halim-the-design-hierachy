# Swiss Modernism 2.0

## Best for
Business, expert, and accessibility-aware audiences who value precision and clarity.

## Core idea
Use grid structure, restrained color, strong alignment, and clean typography to make information feel reliable.

## Above-the-fold layout
- Grid-based composition
- Strong headline aligned to a clear column
- Minimal navigation
- Data, proof, or supporting detail in a structured block

## Visual hierarchy notes
- Let alignment and spacing create order.
- Use type weight instead of decoration.
- Keep color purposeful and limited.

## Works well for
Professional portfolios, product pages, research sites, corporate pages, and editorial explainers.
