# E-Ink / Paper

## Best for
Older adults and audiences who need calm, readable layouts.

## Core idea
Use paper-like surfaces, strong text contrast, minimal movement, and comfortable spacing.

## Above-the-fold layout
- Simple heading
- Large readable paragraph
- Clear navigation
- Calm background
- Obvious action or reading path

## Visual hierarchy notes
- Prioritize legibility over decoration.
- Avoid low-contrast text.
- Keep animations minimal or optional.
- Use predictable structure.

## Works well for
Guides, articles, public information pages, reading apps, healthcare information, and learning resources.
